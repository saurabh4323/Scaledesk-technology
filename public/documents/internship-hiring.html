Internship opportunities — 2026

We are hiring motivated interns who want hands-on exposure to product engineering, modern software delivery, and the discipline of building systems that ship to production. This is a learning-first program with real responsibility—not busywork.

Software Engineering Intern
Product Engineering Intern
AI & Automation Intern
Cloud & DevOps Intern
Data Engineering Intern
Full-Stack Development Intern

Interns are placed on live projects and internal platforms under mentorship from senior engineers. You will participate in standups, code reviews, documentation, and delivery cycles—the same rhythms our full-time teams follow. Duration and mode (remote / hybrid) will be discussed during the selection process.

Eligibility criteria

  • Currently pursuing B.Tech (any discipline) from a recognized university
  • Minimum 65% aggregate in Class 10th (SSC / equivalent)
  • Minimum 65% aggregate in Class 12th (HSC / equivalent)
  • Minimum 65% CGPA / aggregate in current degree program (till latest semester)
  • Expected graduation year 2026, 2027, or 2028
  • Strong communication, ownership, and willingness to learn in a fast-paced engineering environment
  • Available for a minimum internship duration as communicated during onboarding

What you gain

Real engineering exposure

Work on production-minded codebases, client projects, and internal products—not hypothetical assignments.

Mentorship & growth

Learn from experienced engineers through pairing, reviews, and structured feedback on architecture and craft.

Certificate & evaluation

Completion certificate and performance evaluation for eligible interns based on project contribution.

Pre-placement consideration

Outstanding performers may be considered for full-time roles at ScaleDesk Technology.
